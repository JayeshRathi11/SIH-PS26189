DOC_ID: A1 | DOC_TYPE: FORENSIC
Account trace initiated further to a complaint of unauthorized debit registered against a savings account held with a private-sector bank. Records indicate that a sum was moved out via a UPI transaction and credited into an account ending 7743 within minutes of the debit, the transfer logged at approximately 10:15 hrs. From account ending 7743, the funds were further routed, later the same day, into a second account ending 3391, held in a different city, before onward movement could be conclusively traced beyond the audit window covered in this note. Preliminary review of the receiving account's transaction history shows a pattern of frequent small credits from multiple unrelated sources followed by rapid consolidation and outward transfer, a pattern that, taken together with the near-total absence of routine personal-banking activity such as recurring bill payments or salary credits, is consistent with characteristics typically associated with a mule-account arrangement rather than ordinary personal banking activity of the kind ordinarily expected on an individual savings account. The account holder of record for 7743 has not yet been examined in this note; those particulars, along with any prior flags recorded against the account by the bank's own internal monitoring systems, are addressed separately in a supplementary note to follow. No inference is drawn here regarding the method by which the original debit was induced, that aspect falling outside the scope of the present forensic review. Further reconciliation is recommended against the bank's own fraud-monitoring flags for the relevant period, together with a request for extended statements covering the preceding three months on both accounts identified above.
