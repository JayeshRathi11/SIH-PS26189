An FIR was registered at PS Kotwali on the basis of a complaint received from a local trader who stated that he had unknowingly received a fake note in the course of ordinary business, and that on raising the matter with the bank was informed the note was counterfeit. The complaint records that preliminary inquiry, prompted by earlier informant input received by the Economic Offences Cell, indicated that the note in question may have originated from a wider circulation network operating through a fuel-station front in the vicinity, subsequently identified as Chopra Fuel & Service Station.

The complaint further notes that a courier, subsequently identified through informant accounts as one Naseer Ahmed, is believed to move bundles of such notes between distribution points along a route passing through the district, and that the fuel station's proprietor, identified as Vikas Chopra, is suspected of circulating the notes through routine transactions at the station. The narrative records that a coordinator, referred to by associates only as "the source contact," is believed to oversee the arrangement, though no further identifying detail regarding this individual was available at the time of registration.

It is recorded that no operational detail regarding the origin, production, or security features of the notes is reproduced in this narrative, in keeping with standard procedure for matters of this nature. The complaint was formally lodged at the thana at approximately 18:40 hrs, following a preliminary verification exercise conducted earlier in the day. The investigating officer has recommended that surveillance be initiated at the fuel station's known premises, and that telephonic monitoring be sought in respect of any numbers associated with the individuals named in this narrative, subject to appropriate authorisation. No arrests have been effected as of this filing, and the matter has been marked for continued investigation under the relevant provisions, referenced by name only in the formal charge sheet to follow.
