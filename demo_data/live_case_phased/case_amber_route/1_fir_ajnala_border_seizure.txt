An FIR was registered at Ajnala Road Police Station, Amritsar, on receipt of a tip-off from a joint Border Security Force and district police patrol regarding a suspected consignment movement near the Attari border belt. Acting on the information, a police party intercepted a Maruti Ertiga near Ajnala late in the evening and recovered a consignment of heroin wrapped in polythene packets concealed beneath the rear seat, along with four country-made pistols and ammunition packed separately in a canvas bag. Two occupants of the vehicle were detained at the spot and identified as Ranjit Bhullar and Sukhwinder Mann, both residents of villages near Ajnala, who according to preliminary questioning had returned from Malaysia within the preceding two months.

During interrogation, both detained persons stated that they had been operating on instructions relayed through an intermediary from a handler based outside India, and named a local coordinator known to them as "Billa" as the person who assigned pickup points and payment amounts for each consignment. Billa was subsequently identified through local intelligence as Iqbal Deol, a resident of Amritsar city with a prior history of cross-border smuggling cases. Bhullar further stated that a portion of the proceeds from each successful movement was collected in cash by Billa and handed onward to a person referred to as "N.E.C.," understood to be a hawala operator who arranged for the equivalent value to be credited to accounts in Dubai on Billa's instruction, in exchange for a standard commission.

The seizure has been valued by the investigating team at approximately eleven lakh rupees for the narcotics recovered, with the pistols and ammunition assessed separately by the arms wing. Deol has not yet been apprehended and is believed to be avoiding his usual residence. The investigating officer has requested that the call records associated with Bhullar and Mann be obtained for the period preceding the seizure, and that financial intelligence units be alerted to trace any hawala settlement associated with the initials "N.E.C." in connection with this case. The FIR was lodged at approximately 23:40 hrs and the matter has been marked for further investigation under relevant narcotics and arms provisions.
