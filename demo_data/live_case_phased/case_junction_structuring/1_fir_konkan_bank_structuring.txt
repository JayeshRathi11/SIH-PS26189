An FIR was registered at Market Road Police Station following information received from the Financial Intelligence Unit regarding a suspected structuring pattern across multiple savings accounts held at the Konkan Sahakari Bank, Panvel branch. The complainant, an Inspector attached to the Economic Offences Wing, stated that account activity reviewed over the preceding month showed a recurring pattern of small cash deposits, each kept just under the reporting threshold, followed within days by consolidated transfers to a single beneficiary account. The beneficiary account was traced to Vikram Salvi, a resident of Panvel, who according to preliminary enquiry has no declared source of income consistent with the volume of transactions passing through the account.

Further enquiry indicated that the account was opened on the introduction of Sandeep Kale, who is known to local police from an earlier, unrelated matter involving unlicensed betting operations near the Lokhandwala junction. Kale is believed to act as a local coordinator, collecting cash from a network of small bettors and channeling it through accounts such as Salvi's before it is moved onward. Informants further named an individual referred to only as "Bhau" as the person believed to be financing and directing the wider operation, with Kale and Salvi both understood to be acting on Bhau's instructions rather than on their own account. Bhau has separately been linked, through an earlier and unconnected surveillance file, to a small trading concern operating under the name Bhosale Agro Traders, though no direct transaction between that entity and the accounts under review has yet been established.

The complaint was formally lodged at approximately 18:10 hrs. No arrests have been made at the time of this filing, and the matter has been marked for continued financial investigation, with a request made to the bank for the complete transaction history of the accounts named above. The investigating officer has recommended that surveillance be placed on Kale's known movements around the Lokhandwala junction, and that the telephonic contacts associated with both Kale and Salvi be identified and monitored subject to appropriate authorisation.
