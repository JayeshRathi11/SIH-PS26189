Surveillance team stationed near the Lokhandwala junction observed Sandeep Kale arrive on foot at approximately 20:15 hrs and proceed to a tea stall adjacent to the junction, where he was joined roughly ten minutes later by an unidentified male who arrived in a grey Toyota Innova bearing registration MH04CD1234. The two were observed in conversation for approximately fifteen minutes, during which Kale was seen handing over a cloth bag to the second individual, who placed it in the vehicle before departing in the direction of the Panvel bypass. The vehicle was later traced through registration records to Ramesh Bhosale, proprietor of Bhosale Agro Traders, corroborating the earlier informant reference to an individual known as "Bhau."

A subsequent call intercept, obtained under appropriate authorisation, recorded a conversation between Kale and a number registered to Vikram Salvi, in which Kale is heard instructing Salvi to "clear the account by Friday, same as last month," and to expect a further deposit "through the usual boys." A separate, shorter call between Kale and a number later identified as belonging to Ramesh Bhosale recorded Kale confirming that "the collection from the junction crowd" had been handed over, with Bhosale acknowledging receipt and instructing that "the Panvel bank work" be completed before the weekend. Investigators noted that this pattern of activity — cash collection near the Lokhandwala junction, handover to Bhosale personally or through an associate, and onward movement through the Salvi account at Konkan Sahakari Bank, Panvel branch — is consistent with the structuring activity already flagged in the FIR, and provides direct corroboration linking Ramesh Bhosale to the network for the first time. No further individuals were identified during this surveillance window. The matter has been forwarded for continued monitoring, with a recommendation that financial records for Bhosale Agro Traders be sought separately.
