An FIR was registered at PS Yeshwanthpur on the basis of a complaint received from the registered owner of a vehicle bearing registration KA05MN4321, who stated that the vehicle had been found missing from its usual parking location outside his residence. The complainant stated that the vehicle had been parked at the location the previous evening and was discovered missing the following morning, with no indication at the scene of how the vehicle had been taken, a matter left to forensic examination rather than reproduced in this narrative.

The complaint records that preliminary inquiry, prompted by a pattern of similar complaints received by the station in recent months, indicated that the vehicle may have been taken by an individual operating under the name Anil Kamble, believed to be responsible for a series of similar disappearances in the locality. It is recorded that no detail regarding the specific method by which the vehicle was taken is reproduced in this narrative, in keeping with standard procedure for matters of this nature. The vehicle was reported missing at approximately 22:30 hrs the previous evening, per the complainant's account. The investigating officer has recommended that surveillance be initiated at Naidu's known workshop premises, and that coordination be established with the regional RTO to flag any re-registration attempt involving the vehicle's chassis or engine number. No arrests have been effected as of this filing.
